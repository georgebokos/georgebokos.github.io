Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to the FoodDaily app for mobile devices, together with any related services operated by George Bokos (collectively, the "Application"). George Bokos is hereby referred to as the "Service Provider".


What information does the Application obtain and how is it used?

The Application does not collect, log, or store any personal information when you download and use it. Registration is not required. If the Application is used with an active internet connection, technical protocol data (such as your ephemeral IP address) is transmitted to facilitate network connectivity, but this data is not retained or used for tracking.

Subscriptions. The Application offers an optional paid subscription (FoodDaily Premium). If you choose to subscribe, the purchase itself is processed entirely by Google Play; the Service Provider never receives or stores your payment card details. To verify and restore your subscription status, the Application uses RevenueCat, Inc. as a service provider. The following limited data is transmitted to RevenueCat: your purchase history (which product was purchased, and the purchase and expiry dates) and an anonymous identifier generated by the Application for your installation. No name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location are transmitted. This data is used solely to determine whether you have access to paid features.


Does the Application collect precise real time location information of the device?

This Application does not collect precise information about the location of your mobile device.


Do third parties see and/or have access to information obtained by the Application?

If you do not subscribe, no data is shared with third parties.

If you subscribe, the limited subscription data described above is shared with the following providers, solely so that your subscription can be processed and verified:

  • Google Play (Google Ireland Limited) — processes the payment and manages the subscription. See the Google Privacy Policy.
  • RevenueCat, Inc. — verifies purchases and keeps subscription status in sync across your devices. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy.

No data is sold, and none of it is used for advertising or profiling.


What are my opt-out rights?

Since the Application does not collect personal information through normal use, uninstalling it simply removes the Application from your device.

If you contact the Service Provider directly or voluntarily provide information by other means, you may request deletion of that information by contacting fooddaily.app@gmail.com.

Deleting subscription data. To request deletion of the subscription data held by RevenueCat, send an email to fooddaily.app@gmail.com including the user code shown in the Application under Profile & Preferences. Your request will be actioned within 30 days. Note that cancelling a subscription is done through your Google Play account.
